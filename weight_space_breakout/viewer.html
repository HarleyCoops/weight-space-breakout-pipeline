What the policy chose

Probabilities describe the observation before this step. Actions are sampled, so the highest-probability action is not always chosen.

Inside the game

Observation → policy → action → next state + reward

The observation has 500 numbers: 100 cells × 5 channels. The policy produces five action scores. Breaking a brick gives +1; missing the ball gives −1.

Outside the game

LLM → weight edits → game episodes → improvement score

The LLM repairs a small policy. The verifier runs the edited policy on separate evaluation seeds and measures the change in average return.

These are saved replays from the Python simulator, not a second JavaScript implementation. This teaching controller is handwritten, not PPO-trained. Our rules are an independent recreation.
